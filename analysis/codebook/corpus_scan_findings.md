# Corpus Scan Findings — Codebook v0.2 → v1.0

**Scanned:** 59 in-scope JDs across Coal Surface (31), Coal UG (3), Minerals Surface (12), Minerals UG (6), Quarry (7).
**Purpose:** Identify codes and themes that the 10-JD sample missed, and concrete examples of language that doesn't map to v0.2 codes.

---

## Big-picture changes proposed for v1.0

### 1. Rename "COMPETENCY" → split into QUALIFICATIONS + KNOWLEDGE

Recommend a clean split for Libby/Maureen's downstream mapping work:

- **QUALIFICATIONS** = formal credentials (statutory tickets, named training, trades, licenses, RII units). Things a person has been *awarded*.
- **KNOWLEDGE** = "knowledge of X" or "understanding of X" statements. Domain knowledge that doesn't necessarily come from a named credential.

JDs themselves use both terms and the line is sometimes blurry — but for downstream mapping (formal training programs vs. broader competency models), keeping them separate is more useful.

**Result:** 6 categories — DUTY, DISPOSITION, QUALIFICATIONS, KNOWLEDGE, EXPERIENCE, STATUTORY POSITION.

### 2. Many more codes within each category

The 10-JD sample undercounted. Below is a category-by-category list of language I saw in the other 49 JDs that doesn't fit any v0.2 code, with example quotes.

## Specific JD observations worth flagging

1. **JDs with very thin curated content** — Golding.docx, BHP.docx, Golding Contractors.docx have minimal extracted content. The source text has more but extraction is patchy. Coding will need to draw on source text directly for these.

2. **Golding Contractors.docx is a near-perfect "RII unit" JD** — explicitly lists RIIRIS301A, RIIOHS301A, RIICOM301A, RIIRIS402A. Useful exemplar for the Q8 code.

3. **Brightstar Resources is the only JD explicitly naming "Statutory Underground Shift Supervisor ticket"** — distinct from generic shift supervisor.

4. **BQC Supervisor PD is the only JD that uses the formal "POSITION DESCRIPTION" / "Reportable to Site Senior Executive" framing** — and references being "secondary support to SSE and Weighbridge Controller". This is a clear case where the JD points UPWARD to a statutory position (SSE) without itself being one.

5. **MacKellar Leading Hand is borderline scope** — title says "Leading Hand" but body describes a supervisory role. Currently in-scope. Worth confirming with Steph.

6. **Heidelberg Materials is interesting** — Quarry Supervisor but the responsibilities are entirely maintenance-focused (Fixed Plant budget, crushing plants, hydraulic systems, apprentice mentoring). This is a Quarry-sector Maintenance Supervisor by another name. The cross-tab will surface this.

7. **Industry-specific quirks worth noting in the document** — Cert IV TAE appears in 3 JDs; LEAN appears in 1; MSIC only in Rio Weipa; QMS123/G189 only in PYBAR/Brightstar (minerals UG side).
